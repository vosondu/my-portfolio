Why we started this

For months, Albert (a video editor) and I kept getting the same messages from creatives and friends: could we teach what we do?

Most of them had already tried. Downloaded Canva. Opened CapCut. Watched a few tutorials. Started something, and stopped. Not because they couldn’t learn it, but because nobody showed them where to start or what to learn next.

So we did it. In September 2026 we ran our first two free live sessions, and the January cohort builds on what we learned there.

What we’ll cover

🎨

The fundamentals of visual design, and the skills you’ll actually need to develop. That part’s me.

🎬

Video editing

What goes into an engaging video, from the technical cuts to the creative decisions behind them. That part’s Albert.

This is for you if

  • You’re a complete beginner with no experience in design or editing
  • You’ve tried learning before but haven’t made consistent progress
  • You’re overwhelmed by free resources and want a clear starting point
  • You want a useful digital skill, for freelancing, content, or personal growth
  • You’re on PC or mobile. It works on both
Not for you if You’re already working confidently as a designer or editor and looking for advanced technique. This is built for beginners and developing learners.
